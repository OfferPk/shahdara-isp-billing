Unset selling amounts and incomplete package-profit profiles are excluded from their respective totals.

A saved selling price creates one month-specific bill from its effective local month onward; each bill keeps its own price snapshot. Missing eligible months are generated on this device when the app opens, resumes, or remains active across a month boundary—never while it is fully closed. Actual receipts apply first to the selected month; excess automatically credits the next unpaid generated month(s), and any remaining prepaid balance stays attached to its original receipt indefinitely until applied or corrected/deleted. Credit is not a second payment and is shown separately from cash and outstanding due. Correcting or deleting the original receipt recalculates every dependent balance. A bill marked Received closes its due but adds no cash collection. Expected package margin is selling amount minus provider cost, not collected cash profit.
