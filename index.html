RECURRING ESTIMATE

Monthly provider cost by ISP (PKR)

Uses each active profile’s saved monthly purchase cost only. Missing costs are excluded; provider names are never guessed.

    No provider-cost entries have been configured.

    Unset selling amounts and incomplete package-profit profiles are excluded from their respective totals.

    A saved selling price creates one month-specific bill from its effective Pakistan local calendar month; each bill keeps its own price snapshot. Missing eligible months are generated locally when the app opens, resumes, or stays active across a month boundary. Archived customers receive no new bills; unarchiving resumes from the current or next month without backfilling the archived period. Optional due dates are manually set per bill; no default deadline or late penalty is invented. Actual receipts apply first to the selected month; excess automatically credits later unpaid generated bills, and remaining prepaid credit stays valid indefinitely. Credit is not a second payment. Package margin is selling amount minus provider cost; provider costs and margin are expected recurring estimates, not collected cash profit.
